Adds local-voice listening to the extension and published knowledge-base pages.

- Listen to the current page, selected text or latest assistant answer from the extension panel.
- Pause, resume, change speed and local voice, or move between sentences.
- Page and panel playback coordinate so starting one stops the other.
- The published reader remembers position and offers highlighting, seeking and optional next-page continuation.

The FreeDocStore console also adds local English audio generation, preview and download, reviewed recording publication with source-version checks, repository/build diagnostics and printable proposal reviews.

Existing knowledge-base workflows need the current reader installer to enable published-page listening. See the [reader and publishing guide](https://github.com/FreeDocStore/platform/blob/main/docs/READER-AND-PUBLISHING.md) for setup and limits.

Validated with unit tests and Chromium browser checks, including the installed extension and real local model generation. Physical-phone and other browser-engine validation remain follow-up work.
